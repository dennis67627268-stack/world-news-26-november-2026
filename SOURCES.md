## Method notes

- Primary outlets were preferred over recap sites. One Turn’s 26 Nov page mixes multi-day narratives and was used only as a pointer, not as a source of dates.
- Midday market figures (for example ABC’s earlier Hang Seng +2% and Nikkei +1.6% snapshots) were discarded in favour of official cash closes.
- No hype or speculation is stated as fact. Claims (Houthi, unverified port hits, the FT Nvidia report) are tagged.
